The New Yorker's own web fonts, served by www.newyorker.com from its
/.design/fonts directory, converted here to TTF:

  caslon_regular.ttf  TNY Adobe Caslon Pro Regular
  caslon_italic.ttf   TNY Adobe Caslon Pro Italic
  caslon_bold.ttf     TNY Adobe Caslon Pro Bold
  irvin_heading.ttf   TNY Irvin Pro Heading
  graphik_regular.ttf Graphik Regular (the site's Graphik-Regular-Web cut)
  graphik_medium.ttf  Graphik Medium
  neutraface_semibold.ttf Neutraface New Yorker SemiBold

These are the faces the site itself sets text, headlines and the masthead
in. They are © Condé Nast and their founders, and ship with no licence
file; the launcher's icon is likewise the magazine's Eustace Tilly, taken
from the app stores' listings.
