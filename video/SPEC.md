# Videos

- Opens to a list of the videos on the phone, newest first; "No videos found" when empty.
- The focused row is a clearly visible rounded blue card.
- Centre on a row plays it full screen (status bar hidden) with a progress bar and a position line; a paused video says PAUSED in amber.
- Centre pauses/resumes; left/right seek 10 s; up/down seek 60 s; back returns to the list.
- Leaving the app and coming back (Home, a call, screen off) returns to the same video, paused at the same place.
- A video that can't be played shows a toast and returns to the list.
