HBSA for the Sonim XP3

Three ways to read @hbsaasu's posts on a 240×320 screen with a d-pad. Each frame is drawn at the phone's real size and shown at 1.5×. The colours are ASU maroon and gold. Photos are placeholders.

A · Flip-book

One post fills the screen, like a story. Left and Right turn to the next and previous post, Down reads the rest of the caption. If the caption names a date, it shows as a gold tag.

B · AgendaPICKED

A list, newest first, with the next event pinned on top. Each row has the photo, when it was posted and the start of the caption. OK opens the post; in it, dates and times in the caption are highlighted so the when jumps out.

C · Calendar

A month view with a gold dot on every day a caption mentions. The d-pad moves across days; the post for the chosen day shows below. A second tab lists every post.

Why B

B is the simplest. It is a list you scroll and open, which is what every other app on the phone does, and it answers "when is the next event?" before you press a key. A hides how many posts there are and makes you flip through them one at a time to find a date. C is the prettiest, but it rests entirely on reading dates out of captions, and any post without one gets pushed to a second tab. B gets C's best idea (the next event, dates highlighted) without depending on it: if no date is found, the gold card just isn't shown.
