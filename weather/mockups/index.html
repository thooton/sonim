Weather for the Sonim XP3

Mockups at the XP3's 240×320 (mdpi, 1 dp = 1 px), drawn in CARROT's colours: each condition gets CARROT's card colour, sky effect, scenery palette and icon colours. The scene runs edge to edge with no margin; the forecasts below sit straight on the page with a side margin and no cards. Every frame here is drawn by the same layout code, so the numbers in it are the app's.

Keys

Menu (left soft key)Opens the menu: Location, Hourly data, Units, Theme, Refresh.
Up / DownScroll the page two rows of days at a time, down to the last day and no further.
Left / RightScroll the hourly forecast through the next 48 hours.
* and #Previous and next hourly data (Temp, Feels like, Precip chance…), as the iOS chips do.
OKRefresh.
Back (right soft key)Close the menu or dialog; otherwise leave the app.
